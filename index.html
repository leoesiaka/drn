What is Digital Resilience?
Digital resilience is the ability of individuals, organisations, and society to anticipate, withstand, adapt to, and recover from digital disruptions — whether caused by cyberattacks, technology failures, or other threats to our connected infrastructure.

Who we are

We collaborate to bridge research, policy, and practice towards advancing UK digital resilience.

Digital Resilience Network UK (DRN UK)
Digital Resilience Network UK (DRN UK) is an independent, UK-focused community hub and network dedicated to contributing to the strengthening of digital resilience across public services, organisations, and communities in the UK.
Who we bring together
Powered by Black professionals and open to all, DRN UK welcomes everyone who believes digital resilience must be deliberately built into the people, processes and technologies that underpin our modern life, working together in the public interest.
